Everyone I've Ever Known

About This Archive

This is an ongoing archive of people, created by Graham Hooper and his son Oliver.
It was started in the late summer of 2026, with individuals remembered and added one human at a time.

It might be fun to see if I know you, guessing from clues here. But it can be just as interesting to explore the site, thinking about the sets of facts, as presented, imagining the real folk behind the statements.
